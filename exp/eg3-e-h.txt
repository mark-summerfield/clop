usage: str extract [OPTIONS] [@GID] <FILE1> … <FILEn>

Extract the given filenames at the generation, e.g., filename.ext
will be extracted as filename@gid.ext, etc.

POSITIONALS: 
  @GID is the generation to extract [default current]; FILE1 …
  FILEn are the files to extract.

OPTIONS:
  -v or --verbose  Show the name of every extractd file [default just
                   show counts].
  -h or --help     Show extract help and quit.
